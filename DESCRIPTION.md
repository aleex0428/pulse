Feel the driver's heart. Every driver gets a heart rate that lives with the race. It climbs on the grid, surges at lights out, rises in a fight, jumps after contact and comes down behind the safety car. You **feel** yours on screen and **see** it on a bedside monitor.

### The heart
- Worked out from what the car does, read from the game's memory (no UDP needed): the session, the start, the pace and the G, the nearest car (defending counts more than attacking) and what is at stake.
- Spikes of adrenaline from contact, spins and trips off the road, places lost and gained, track limits and penalties.
- The safety car, a red flag, the pit lane and the flag calm it. Fatigue builds over a long, hard race.
- Zones: CALM, FOCUSED, IN THE ZONE and the RED ZONE.

### On screen
- **Tunnel vision** in the red zone and a **heartbeat** throb at the edges.
- **G greyout** in fast corners and under hard braking.
- The **flash and blur** of an impact.
- **Fatigue** late in a long race.
- The centre of the picture always stays clear, and nothing shows while paused, in replays or in menus.

### Monitor, card and report
- A **bedside monitor** with the heart rate, the zone, a sweeping ECG trace and the stamina left. **F9** shows and hides it.
- In a race, a TV-style **DRIVER BIOMETRICS** card compares your heart with your rival's when you fight or touch.
- At the flag, a **PULSE REPORT**: your zone control score, your peak heart rate and what caused it, your average, the time in each zone and your stamina.

### Settings
Find these in **ADDON SETTINGS**.
- Effects strength, a Comfort style (no throb, blur, flash or grain), Screen motion (Full, Reduced or Static), and each effect on its own.
- An optional key to switch the effects off and on while you drive.
- Where the monitor sits and how big it is, and its key.
- The card and the report.

### Needs
Swofty Launcher 1.9.10 or later. Steam.
